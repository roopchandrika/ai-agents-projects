# Northwind Employee Handbook

Northwind Robotics gives every employee 24 days of paid time off per year, plus the office closure between
December 24 and January 1. PTO requests go through the Workday portal at least two weeks ahead for anything longer
than three days.

We run "Flexday Fridays": on the second Friday of each month there are no meetings, and teams use the day for
learning, cleanup and side projects. Remote work is allowed up to three days a week; the Pittsburgh assembly floor
requires badge-in on shift days.

The learning budget is $2,500 per person per year for courses, books and conferences. Expense it through Ramp
with the receipt and a one-line note on what you learned.
