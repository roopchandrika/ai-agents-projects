# Northwind Product Overview

Northwind builds autonomous mobile robots for warehouses. Our flagship product, PalletPilot, moves pallets up to
1,200 kg between receiving docks and racking without fixed tracks, using lidar and fiducial markers on the floor.

PickMate is the smaller tote-carrying robot, launched in 2025, designed to work alongside human pickers. Both
robots are coordinated by the Northwind Fleet Manager software, sold as an annual subscription per robot.

Customers include regional grocery distributors and third-party logistics providers across North America.
