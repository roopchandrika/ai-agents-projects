# Northwind Salary Bands 2026

Salary bands for 2026, effective March 1. Engineering levels: E3 $118,000 to $142,000; E4 $140,000 to $171,000;
E5 (senior) $168,000 to $205,000; E6 (staff) $195,000 to $212,000.

Operations technicians: T1 $52,000 to $61,000; T2 $60,000 to $72,000. Managers are paid at the top of the band of
the level they manage plus 8%.

The annual merit pool is 3.5% of payroll. Offers above the band maximum need CFO approval.
