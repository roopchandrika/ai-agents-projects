# Northwind Engineering Roadmap 2027

Project Kestrel is our top engineering priority for 2027: a PalletPilot variant that can operate outdoors in
yards and loading areas, targeting rain and light snow. Prototype field trials start in Q2 2027 at a customer site
in Columbus.

The second priority is replacing the lidar supplier. The current vendor's unit costs $3,900; the new solid-state
candidate is quoted at $1,450 in volume, which would cut PalletPilot's bill of materials by about 11%.

Fleet Manager will move to a new planning engine that handles 500 robots per site, up from 150 today.
