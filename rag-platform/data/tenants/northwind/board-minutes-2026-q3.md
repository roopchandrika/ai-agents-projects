# Northwind Board Minutes Q3 2026

Board meeting, September 12, 2026. Present: full board and CEO. The board discussed an unsolicited acquisition
approach from Atlas Dynamics valuing Northwind at $410 million. The board agreed not to engage before the Series C
closes and asked the CEO to retain Morgan Keller as financial advisor.

The board approved a Series C target of $60 million led by existing investors. Headcount plan for 2027 was
approved at 240 employees, up from 185.

Next meeting: December 5, 2026.
