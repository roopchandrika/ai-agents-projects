# Helix Pipeline Overview

Helix develops antibody therapies for autoimmune disease. HX-201, our lead candidate, targets the IL-23
pathway for moderate-to-severe psoriasis and is in a Phase 2 trial across 40 sites.

HX-305 is a bispecific antibody for lupus, currently in IND-enabling studies. Our discovery platform combines
phage display with machine-learning models that predict antibody developability.

Helix was founded in 2019 and has about 160 employees in Boston and San Diego.
