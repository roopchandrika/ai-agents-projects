# Helix Board Minutes Q3 2026

Board meeting, September 19, 2026. The board reviewed a proposed licensing deal with Corvane Pharma for
HX-305 ex-US rights: $45 million upfront and up to $600 million in milestones. The board authorized management
to negotiate a term sheet.

Interim HX-201 Phase 2 data showed PASI-75 response in 64% of patients at week 16 versus 11% on placebo. The
board approved planning for Phase 3, subject to the FDA end-of-Phase-2 meeting.

Cash runway: 26 months at current burn.
