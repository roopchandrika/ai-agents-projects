# Helix Salary Bands 2026

2026 compensation bands. Scientist I $98,000 to $118,000; Scientist II $115,000 to $139,000; Senior Scientist
$136,000 to $165,000; Principal Scientist $160,000 to $187,400.

Research associates: RA I $62,000 to $74,000; RA II $72,000 to $86,000. Equity refresh grants are awarded
annually at 25% of the new-hire grant for the level.

Relocation packages are capped at $15,000.
