# Helix Data Platform Roadmap 2027

Project Meridian replaces our assay data pipelines with a single lakehouse on Databricks by Q3 2027, so
that screening results reach the ML models within an hour instead of three days.

The second initiative is an in-house developability model trained on 18,000 antibodies from our historical
campaigns; the goal is to cut wet-lab screening rounds from four to two.

Budget request: $2.3 million, including four new data engineering hires.
