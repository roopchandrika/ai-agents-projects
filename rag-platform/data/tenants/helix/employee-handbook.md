# Helix Employee Handbook

Helix Biotherapeutics offers 20 days of paid time off, 10 sick days and a paid week off between Christmas and
New Year. Lab staff schedule time off through the lab manager so that cell cultures are always covered.

Once a quarter we hold Science Saturday, an optional half-day where teams present results to families and
friends. The Boston site is badge-access only; guests must be registered in Envoy a day ahead.

Every employee receives a $1,800 annual wellness stipend for gym memberships, therapy or equipment.
