# Summit Freight Cargo Claims Report

Cargo claims through August 2026 totaled $2.9 million, a claims ratio of 1.4% of revenue, above our 1.0%
target. Two thirds of the dollar value came from temperature excursions in the new refrigerated service.

The Salt Lake City terminal accounts for 31% of damage claims, linked to forklift handling on the night shift.

Actions: reefer units get remote temperature alerts, and SLC night-shift forklift operators will be retrained.
