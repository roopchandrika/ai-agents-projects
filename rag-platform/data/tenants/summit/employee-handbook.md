# Summit Freight Employee Handbook

Summit Freight employees accrue 1.5 days of PTO per month. Drivers bid for time off by seniority through
the dispatch app; office staff use the HR portal.

Drivers with 12 months without a preventable incident earn the Road Ready bonus of $1,000. Company trucks are
equipped with dash cameras that record only when the truck is in motion.

The Denver headquarters has free parking and a subsidized cafeteria.
