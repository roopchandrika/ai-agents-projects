# Summit Freight Board Minutes Q3 2026

Board meeting, September 26, 2026. Management presented a proposal to acquire Ridgeline Carriers, a
regional carrier with 11 terminals in Idaho and Montana, for $74 million. The board approved due diligence
with a target signing in Q1 2027.

The ESOP trustee reported the annual share valuation at $41.20 per share, up from $38.75.

The board asked HR to present a driver retention plan at the next meeting.
