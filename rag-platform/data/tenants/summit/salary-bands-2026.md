# Summit Freight Pay Scales 2026

2026 pay scales. Linehaul drivers: $0.62 per mile in the first year rising to $0.68 per mile after five years.
City drivers: $29.50 to $36.00 per hour. Dock workers: $22.00 to $27.50 per hour.

Office roles: dispatcher $58,000 to $70,000; terminal manager $92,000 to $118,000.

The 2026 general increase is 3% effective April 1.
