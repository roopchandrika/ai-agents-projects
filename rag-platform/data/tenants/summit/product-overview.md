# Summit Freight Services

Summit Freight is a less-than-truckload carrier serving 14 states in the Mountain West, with 38 terminals and
about 900 tractors.

Customers book and track shipments through SummitTrack, our online portal, which shows real-time location and
estimated delivery windows. Temperature-controlled service launched in 2025 for food and pharmaceutical
shippers.

Summit is employee-owned through an ESOP established in 2016.
